QUICK GUIDE

Using ResearchOS

Pick a project, then give the team a focused question. Add the research goal, data, methods, and success criteria when you know them.

  1. Connect a local modelOpen Model settings and choose an installed Ollama model or connect llama.cpp.
  2. Find a specific paperUse Find papers to search Crossref, Europe PMC, and OpenAlex. Inspect results without saving; save only the records you want to keep.
  3. Start an investigationResearch runs search trusted scholarly indexes and attach source labels to their report findings.
  4. Follow the workOpen Agent team for the live journal, then Research runs for the source-linked report. Use the report chat for follow-up questions.
  5. Analyze one paper in depthOpen PDF analysis, add a paper, then choose Open paper analysis and Analyze full paper. Findings link to exact quoted PDF pages; ask follow-up questions in Paper Q&A.

Your papers and models stay in the local data folder. The startup launcher handles the app setup and opens the browser for you.

OPTIONAL

A scientific-paper embedding model that reorders trusted-index results by research relevance. It does not write reports or replace your chat model. About 450 MB of weights plus the optional PyTorch runtime; everything is stored under this app’s local data folder.
